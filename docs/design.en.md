# Design and implementation / v0.3

This document describes implementation. See [product design](../PRODUCT_DESIGN.md) for product scope, visual style and Android interaction requirements.

## 0.3.0 expanded tracks and optional maps

`core/TrackGeometry.kt` produces presentation geometry without changing recorded analysis. It validates original adjacent fixes, refusing to bridge invalid points, jumps or gaps exceeding 15 seconds. Speeds follow existing moving/waiting/unknown segments, using displacement only when it exceeds positioning uncertainty. Unknown speed remains gray; a fixed red-to-green scale covers 0/20/40/60+ km/h. Reference routes stay monochrome. Labels use confirmed WAITING intervals of at least 30 seconds and median valid coordinates; revisits remain separate. Isolated jumps do not enlarge fitted bounds.

`ui/TrackViews.kt` supplies previews, a full-screen Dialog and independent local Canvas with pinch, pan and fit controls. Recording reuses `RecordingService.latestFix` and freshness checks without another location provider. Native maps exist only in the visible recording preview or an expanded view; opening the live expanded view removes the preview's native map. Missing keys, disabled maps, offline access, SDK/authentication failure and loading timeout preserve local viewing. Both renderers share geometry.

`maps/MapSettings.kt` defaults off and accepts each APK user's Android SDK AK and published style ID. A separate Keystore AES-GCM key protects the AK; credentials never enter SavedState, logs or Room. Saving with maps enabled accepts the linked SDK privacy policy. `BaiduRuntime.ensure` runs only when an enabled/configured map is viewed; neither Application nor the recording Service initializes the SDK. Maps do not supply recording location, route recognition or statistics. Authentication failures expose no raw SDK messages and allow fallback/retry. Settings copy this APK's package and SHA1 for AK registration.

`BaiduTrackController` owns TextureMapView and host resume/pause/destroy. Official CoordinateConverter converts raw WGS84 to display-only BD09LL, cached in memory. Continuous polylines are split into at most 1000 vertices; stable chunks are reused and changed tails updated. Stop labels are culled by screen collision and duration. SDK branding/scale remain visible, with POIs, buildings, indoor maps and live traffic off by default. A published Tea Field or other style ID loads online and falls back to the minimal basemap on failure.

The expanded view's Display panel persists minimal (no 3D buildings), standard, satellite and custom basemaps, plus speed colors, stops, endpoints, place names and live traffic. `MapAppearance` shares a persistence mutex with credentials but writes its own preference fields without re-encrypting or overwriting the AK. Existing style IDs default to Custom on migration. Track choices apply to local Canvas as well; basemap controls affect Baidu only and add no modules. Live traffic is identified as current road conditions, not historical speeds.

`StopCalloutPainter` shares 11 sp rounded bubbles, leaders and location dots. `StopCalloutLayout` searches eight directions at 16/32/48 dp gaps. Candidate bodies must avoid the real track edges plus 5 dp clearance, other bubbles, endpoint/current/stop markers and viewport edges. Only `TrackGeometry.edges` are indexed, without bridging gaps. Edges are clipped near the viewport and bucketed in 64 dp screen cells, bounding searches on long/zoomed trips. If none fits, only the bubble is hidden; up to 30 bubbles are shown.

Native bitmap bounds include the complete bubble and leader. Each Marker's relative anchor always references the true stop, including sideways/below placements. Bitmaps are reused within the same slot and recycled/replaced when direction or gap changes. Markers move with geographic coordinates during gestures and are laid out again at gesture completion. Local expanded maps share layout and drawing; stop coordinates and analysis stay unchanged. `MapAppearance.forSurface` temporarily suppresses stops in previews without changing the saved expanded-map preference.

Version is 0.3.0 with internal versionCode 16, supporting replacement of installed development builds. Room stays at v4 with no migration. Dependencies are limited to map/coordinate utilities and required base modules, excluding navigation, Baidu location, track cloud and traffic signals. Default APKs include arm64-v8a and compressed native libraries; `rideDiaryAbis` selects other architectures. Online maps process location/area and device information under SDK policy, superseding early-version statements about having no map API.

## 0.1.8 weather and recommendation implementation

`weather/` owns an independent WeatherViewModel, HTTPS client, private disk cache, Keystore settings and directional recommendations. Queries run while Record is composed and the Activity is at least STARTED; leaving Record/backgrounding stops new work. In-flight blocking requests have timeouts and cancellation prevents subsequent requests. Check every 15 minutes, cache for 20 minutes and bypass cache on manual refresh; failures may retain data up to six hours old, without invented values. At most three concurrent requests run; a Mutex deduplicates provider/credential-digest/language/rounded-point keys. Keep at most 60 digest-named cache files. Weather is display-only and never changes trip statistics.

Open-Meteo `/v1/forecast` bundles current, two-day hourly and daily data using Unix UTC times, automatic timezone and m/s. QWeather uses the personal Host’s `/weather/v1/current`, `hourly?hours=48` and `daily?days=2&localTime=true`, with an `X-QW-Api-Key` header. Parse minute-resolution ISO times, probabilities in 0–1 as percentages and daily local offsets. These are client integrations; no live calls or real-key integration checks have run.

Accept only HTTPS subdomains of `qweatherapi.com`, disable redirects, set seven-second connect/read timeouts and limit decompressed responses to 2 MB. Never log error URLs, response bodies, coordinates or keys. Android Keystore AES-GCM encrypts the key in private preferences; masked input never enters SavedState. Saving provider/off changes updates configuration and clears cache; turning weather off prevents new requests. Show separate 401/403, 429, network and data failures, plus Open-Meteo/CC BY 4.0 or QWeather-returned attribution links.

Use a fresh existing recording fix or foreground one-shot location (approximate access accepted, ten-second timeout). Explicitly label recent route-origin fallback. Weather permission is separate and never starts recording. No maps, geocoding or background weather jobs are involved; only two-decimal coordinates leave the device, never full tracks.

`RouteSuggestions` uses up to 20 latest usable samples per direction from 60 days. Rank frequency, recency, circular departure-time distance and morning/afternoon direction preference; use weekday/weekend subsets when at least three exist. Candidates are actual existing directional references. Fewer than three samples is a reference time; no samples never becomes a fabricated usual time. Cards combine endpoint hourly forecasts from departure through median duration (one hour without history), with stale/partial coverage labels. Endpoint forecasts cannot describe every point along the route.

Quick start carries `EXTRA_ROUTE_ID` through an Intent and binds reference/direction in a transaction, retaining pending permission selection in SavedState. The Service retains explicit selection for finish, restricting candidates and preserving the association; incomplete/deviated rides remain auto-excluded. Generic start keeps full-track automatic matching. Process death still marks interruption without resuming, retaining persisted routeId. Unavailable choices produce a dedicated error. Room remains v2 without migration; screen-awake logic is unchanged.

Official references: [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs), [QWeather current](https://dev.qweather.com/docs/api/weather/weather-current/), [hourly](https://dev.qweather.com/docs/api/weather/weather-hourly-forecast/), [daily](https://dev.qweather.com/docs/api/weather/weather-daily-forecast/), [key authentication](https://dev.qweather.com/docs/configuration/authentication/), [API Host](https://dev.qweather.com/docs/configuration/api-host/), reviewed 2026-10-23. Follow attribution and account terms; non-commercial free access is not unlimited commercial access.

## 0.1.6 route and finish contract

- **Route before direction:** Select a route first: the overview below the route selector and above direction tabs combines both directions for valid distance, commute days and ride count in the selected month. Charts and duration metrics below the tabs use To work / To home separately, without cross-route aggregation. Routes shows one card per name with separate directional samples and tracks.
- **Logical route versus actual reference:** groupId identifies the named route; each direction retains actual geometry, endpoint radii and historical links. Creating the same name reuses the group, while existing directions are selected rather than recreated. Migration normalizes trailing direction labels, whitespace and English case to group legacy references, preserving IDs and raw records.
- **Every finish requires review:** manual stop, notification finish and arrival detection open Cancel / Delete / Save. Cancel or Back continues; Delete stops and removes the current ride and raw points; only Save completes and retains it. Timing and location continue during review. Arrival prompts once; after cancelling, manual finish remains available.
- **Classification after saving:** match the complete track again. When a group has only one direction and the complete reverse trace clearly matches it, create the missing reference from this ride's actual points. A reversed reference is inference only, never stored as fabricated travel. Ambiguous groups/directions remain unassigned.
- **Correction:** saving opens the route list, also reachable via Select or correct route in details. Create new route is the first item; existing routes expose both directions. Selecting a missing direction opens a form locked to that group name and creates it from the current ride. Dismissing correction does not undo the saved ride.
- **Management:** rename all directions together; edit endpoint radii for the selected reference. Deleting a group unlinks rides and retains history/raw fixes. A group in active use is protected. Short tracks can be saved or classified, but cannot create a reference.
- **Android interaction:** use Material 3 dialogs, scrolling lists and bottom forms. Direction has text and selected semantics as well as color. Back closes overlays and keeps recording. Preserve the palette, graph-paper logo and motorcycle progress. Large text, TalkBack and landscape still require broader coverage.

Room v2 explicitly migrates v1 with groupId and arrivalSuggested. ETA continues using strictly earlier histories of the concrete directional reference, avoiding position comparisons between different geometries. The user has verified screen-awake behavior; it is not retested in this iteration. Recording remains active during finish review.

## Structure and lifecycle

One Android application module, Kotlin, Compose and Material 3. `core/` contains pure deterministic geometry, segmentation, route recognition, historical statistics. `data/` owns Room entities, transactions and a mutex-protected repository. `recording/` owns replaceable `LocationSource`, Android `LocationManager` and a foreground service. `ui/` owns the ViewModel, four screens, trace/motorcycle Canvas and correction dialogs. The ViewModel observes Room with Flow; the service owns recording and does not depend on a visible UI or Activity instance.

Start is a user action in the visible Activity, after precise location permission. The service immediately posts its foreground notification, waits for database recovery, then allocates one ride and subscribes to GNSS/network providers at a requested 2-second interval. A selector prefers good GNSS fixes for 10 seconds, falls back to network, suppresses redundant sub-second callbacks, and lets a good source replace a poor one. This prevents concurrent providers from creating artificial jump/unknown intervals. Requests are serialized on a service-owned channel; repeated starts return without allocating another session. Location and timer checkpoints transact raw fixes, ride summary state and derived segments. The timer continues when fixes disappear. Notification finish opens Activity review; confirmed Save/Delete are serialized service commands. `START_NOT_STICKY` prevents accidental recording resurrection. There is no boot receiver or background-location permission. Android may vary delivery intervals; 2 seconds is a request, not a reliability promise.

On a new process, existing RECORDING rows become INTERRUPTED, with the wall-clock gap since the last checkpoint added as unknown. Old monotonic points are not connected to a new clock origin. This gap is explicitly uncertain (and can be distorted by clock changes); the record stays out of valid history and can be inspected/trimmed without deleting raw data. The app does not resume a killed ride. A normal live ride uses `elapsedRealtime`, so wall-clock changes cannot change its elapsed total.

## Data and privacy

`rides`: original UTC start/end, recording timezone, persisted elapsed time and last save wall time, state, route/direction, exclusion flag/reason, algorithm version, optional corrected commute boundaries, confirmed-arrival flag. `points`: ordered raw fixes with UTC, ride-relative elapsed time, WGS84 coordinate system, accuracy, nullable speed/speed accuracy and quality. `segments`: derived moving/stationary/unknown intervals with distance; replaceable from raw points. `routes`: source ride ID, ordered reference points, independent direction, endpoint radii and version.

Trips and full tracks stay local. Optional weather uses Internet access with rounded coordinates; there are no trackers, precise coordinate logs, map APIs or GMS. Backup/cloud transfer exclusions are in the manifest and extraction rules. Tracks use app-private storage without additional application encryption. SharedPreferences stores language, weather preferences and Keystore-encrypted QWeather credentials. There is no destructive database migration fallback. A future schema change must include a migration and retention test. Raw fixes and original timestamps remain after edits; UI metrics are recalculated on selected boundaries rather than accumulated monthly counters.

## Analysis version 1.0

Thresholds are centralized in `Policy`. Accuracy must be ≤35 m; a source fix older than 10 seconds is tagged stale. Intervals >15 seconds, invalid endpoints or implied speed >55 m/s are unknown and contribute no distance. Speed with reported uncertainty >2 m/s is ignored. If speed is absent, infer it only when displacement exceeds mean endpoint accuracy; otherwise classify the interval unknown. Never convert missing speed to zero. Invalid raw points are retained, not joined across for distance.

Low speed below 0.7 m/s is a stationary candidate. After 12 seconds, backfill that candidate interval as stationary and erase its drift distance. Exit stationary at 1.4 m/s. Merge contiguous classified intervals; stop counts/longest/mean derive from merged stationary intervals. Slow movement beginning above the entry threshold remains moving. Hysteresis can still classify some very slow movement after a stop as stationary; field calibration is necessary. Unconfirmed short candidates remain moving. Total = moving + stationary + unknown. Stationary share uses total elapsed. Overall speed = valid distance / total elapsed; moving speed uses moving elapsed. Unknown >20% or no elapsed hides speed and excludes valid history.

Route creation uses the selected valid recorded trace, minimum 150 m and four fixes, endpoint radius 20–500 m (defaults 100/70). Recognition needs departure within the start radius, at least four valid observations, ≥120 m ordered progress and ≥75% corridor inliers. Each point projects onto reference line segments inside a local window: at most 60 m behind and a displacement- and time/speed-limited 60–250 m ahead (with a start-radius allowance for the first fix). A small advance penalty discourages distant crossing branches. End residual >25 m or backtracking >30 m indicates deviation/turnback; live attribution persists for manual correction. Saving re-evaluates the full trace and clears an unconfirmed attribution. Near-equal candidates (residual margin <10 m) remain matching. Clock departure time is not a rejection criterion; time-window optimization is deferred.

Progress is projected along the ordered reference distance, smoothed against small backward drift and capped at 99% until arrival. Deviation or stale location suppresses live progress and ETA. Actual arrival requires ≥92% progress, all recent fixes within the end radius, ≥3 fresh fixes spanning 30 seconds with no >15-second gap, and speed <0.7 m/s. Arrival sets arrivalSuggested and opens review; only explicit Save completes the ride and permits the 100% arrival summary indicator. Passing an endpoint or a short stop does not suffice. This is a heuristic, not map matching that can guarantee adjacent-road identification. One route containing large missing sections can be a poor reference; inspect its sketch before using it. Manual finish and correction are available. A manually finished matched ride with less than 92% progress is automatically excluded from baselines; review/correction can restore it.

## History and aggregation

Only completed, non-excluded, classified rides with ≤20% unknown and at least 150 m valid distance enter valid history/statistics. Baselines require same route and direction, strict UTC start before the current ride, a different ID, newest 20 samples maximum. At least three samples are required for conclusions/ETA. Total, moving and stationary medians are independent. The handoff example remains two distinct comparisons: today 29:36 vs total median 32:10 gives −02:34; moving +01:00 plus stationary −03:12 gives −02:12 against component medians.

ETA projects each historical trace to the current along-route position, takes its observed remaining elapsed time, and adds median/25th–75th percentile remaining time to now. The UI also compares current elapsed to historical elapsed at that position and reports actual sample count. Crossing times use the first sample reaching the position (2-second sampling approximation). No live traffic or paid API. No fake ETA before classification or with too few usable historical projections.

Monthly statistics select by departure local date in the stored zone, route and direction. Chart and table use the same filtered set. Days are unique dates; two rides on one date are one day/two rides. Clocks use circular anchoring and unwrap across midnight before mean/min/max; a near-uniform whole-day distribution uses the first observed minute as anchor. This is intended for commute windows, not uniformly distributed 24-hour activity. Arrival clock uses stored end UTC; boundary correction derives corrected UTC from original start plus elapsed offset, so changing the system clock mid-ride can make corrected clock labels approximate. Zero wait and one sample are legal; one sample is labeled.

## 0.2.2 stop and location-status repair

A main-thread RecordingBuffer retains raw fixes until persistence succeeds. A CONFLATED channel coalesces only wakeups, never raw fixes. Stop immediately freezes monotonic/UTC cutoff and stops collection/ticks. After any in-flight batch, one final batch drains retained fixes and completes the ride. Failed writes retain the batch and original stop decision for explicit retry. Database version remains 4; historical rides are not corrected.

RouteShape lazily caches projection edges and length. Binary search selects candidate edges while preserving the original local window, scoring, ties and short-edge rules. LiveRide calculates trace/route/ETA through a background produceState, independently of clock ticks. Location freshness uses acquisition monotonic timestamps and quality instead of stale DB snapshots; genuinely poor/stale fixes still show quality warnings.

### 0.2.2 route reacquisition and candidate diagnostics

Routing.evaluate exposes candidates before formal confirmation. Overlap is the share of collected usable fixes passing the 25 m corridor, ordering and continuity checks, not whole-route progress or confidence. Confirmation still needs four fixes, 120 m of matched movement and 75% overlap. Failed tracking expands a bounded 250–1200 m search according to observed movement, requiring three ordered continuous fixes, 15 m displacement and 10 m reference advance to reacquire. Long GNSS gaps do not imply travel. Short backward jitter no longer permanently latches failure; actual U-turns/current deviation still suppress progress and ETA. Similar candidates in distinct groups/directions remain ambiguous.

The repository publishes per-ride ephemeral StateFlow reports after successful transactions. The UI reuses these reports. Raw fixes/segments persist independently; matching runs only for new points after the default 5 s interval, adapting to four times the prior computation cost up to 15 s. Route/binding changes invalidate the cache and finish always forces review. Below the main recording card, a compact muted section shows up to two route/direction candidates and overlap, without a separate card, progress bars, status explanations or update times. Hide this auxiliary section when no candidates or usable location exist; route attribution appears only in the main card title, without repeated matching/ambiguous/unmatched labels. Location status shares the recording-title row, keeping muted 14 sp text with a green dot for normal and a red dot for abnormal status; short labels cover weak signal, waiting, location disabled and permission issues. Reports are not persisted and old rides are not retrospectively reclassified.
